# "Easy" — Class 10 History, operational definition

Easy = recall of a fact, name, date, or single-point identification.
NOT "explain", NOT "analyse", NOT multi-step reasoning.

## Anti-examples (MEDIUM — reject in easy tier)
- "Explain the causes of..." (multi-point)
- "Analyse the impact of..." (reasoning)
- "Compare X and Y" (synthesis)
- "To what extent..." (evaluation)

## Question Type Scope (Phase 3 vs Phase 4)
The 9 target question types were triaged by cognitive level:
- **Phase 3 (Easy, single-chunk grounding):** MCQ, 2m Short Answer, Source-based, Chronology.
- **Phase 4 (Medium/Hard, multi-chunk synthesis):** Compare/Contrast, Inference, Assertion-Reasoning, Justification, Analysis.
